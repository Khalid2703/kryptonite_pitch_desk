Root Cause
The current tools measure
the wrong thing.
Pitch Decks
Measure storytelling ability and presentation polish — not how a founder acts when the company is burning at 3am.
Reference Calls
Subjective, slow, socially biased, and structurally inconsistent across candidates. Not comparable at scale.
Interviews
Measure interview skill. Founders who are great under structured questioning often collapse under real operational pressure.
Track Record
Only works for repeat founders. First-time founders — who need capital most — have no measurable signal.
The Gap
There is no standardized, AI-powered, pressure-tested, comparable signal for founder decision-making quality. Until now.
The Solution
A 20-minute simulation that reveals
how founders actually think.
1

Founder enters a hyper-personalized startup crisis

Google Gemini 2.5 Flash generates a scenario calibrated to the founder's industry, stage, runway, team size, and declared archetype. No two scenarios are the same.

2

They respond in open text — no multiple choice

Founders articulate their full strategic reasoning. No coached answers. No A/B/C/D. Gemini evaluates the quality of their thinking, not pattern-matching.

3

AI produces a structured Cognitive Assessment Report

Scores across 5 dimensions with a percentile rank against the full founder cohort. Shareable with investors as a verified due diligence signal.

4

Socratic loop closes the learning cycle

A follow-up question forces the founder to defend their reasoning — exposing deeper patterns. Every answer is logged in the permanent Cognitive Ledger.
